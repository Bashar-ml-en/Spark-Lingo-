AI & Voice Notice

How Sparky AI and voice features process your content

Status: DRAFT pending legal counsel approval. Do not rely on this text until the final version is published.

What happens when you use Sparky AI

  • Your chat messages are sent to our server and forwarded to the Google Gemini language model to generate a reply.
  • Your conversation history is stored in your account so Sparky can continue where you left off.
  • Nothing you type is used to train any model.
  • AI output can contain mistakes. Practice results are never official scores.

What happens when you use voice input

  • Audio you record is uploaded to our server solely to transcribe it into text for your practice session.
  • Recordings are not stored as permanent account data and are not shared with third parties.

Your controls

AI features operate only after you accept this notice (recorded server-side). You may withdraw consent in Settings; AI features then become unavailable until you consent again.
