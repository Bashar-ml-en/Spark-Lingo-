Managing your Spark Lingo subscription

Where subscriptions are managed

Spark Lingo subscriptions are billed through the store you installed from:

  • Google Play: Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Apple App Store: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Cancellation

Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep access until the end of the current paid period.

Restore purchases

Use "Restore purchases" in the app after reinstalling or switching devices.
