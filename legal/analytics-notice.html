Analytics Notice

Effective 30 August 2026 · Version 1.0.0 — Optional product analytics and crash reporting

What we collect if you opt in

  • Product events: lesson completions (with lesson ID and score), card review outcomes, AI lesson requests (level and language).
  • Crash reports via Firebase Crashlytics when the app encounters a serious error.

What we never do

  • We never link these events to your app account ID or email.
  • We never collect audio, voice content, or chat message text through analytics.

Your controls

Analytics is OFF by default. Enabling it requires your affirmative consent. You can disable it any time in Settings; collection stops immediately.
