Spark Lingo ("the App") is a language-learning application. Contact for privacy matters: abulithbisha@gmail.com.

3. What we do NOT collect

  • No precise location data
  • No contacts, calendar, or health data
  • No advertising identifiers; no third-party ad SDKs
  • Analytics and crash collection starts disabled and stays off until you grant consent recorded on our servers

4. Consent

AI processing requires your consent, recorded server-side, before Sparky AI features operate. Analytics/crash telemetry requires a separate affirmative opt-in. You can withdraw consent at any time; collection stops immediately.

6. Your rights

  • Access: your progress and chat history are visible in the app.
  • Deletion: deleting your account permanently removes your account and all associated data.
  • Contact: email us for any privacy request.

7. Security

All data travels over HTTPS/TLS. AI endpoints validate your session token and enforce rate limits. Secrets live server-side only; no service credentials ship in the app.

8. Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Changes

Material changes will be announced in-app before taking effect.
