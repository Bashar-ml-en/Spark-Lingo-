## 2. What we collect
- **Account information**: email address, and/or Google OAuth profile
  data (name, email) if you sign in with Google.
- **Learning data**: your progress, flashcard review history (SM-2
  state), unit/lesson completion, exam-readiness estimates and mock
  exam scores.
- **Audio recordings**: speech you record during AI tutor (Sparky)
  sessions and speaking-section mock exams, used to transcribe and
  score your practice.
- **Device/diagnostic data**: crash reports and performance metrics via
  Firebase Crashlytics.
- **Purchase data**: subscription/purchase status via RevenueCat (we do
  not see your payment card details — these are handled by Apple/
  Google's payment systems).

## 3. How we use it

scheduling, AI conversation practice, exam-readiness scoring), to

subscription.
